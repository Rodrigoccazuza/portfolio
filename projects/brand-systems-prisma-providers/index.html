04, Guidelines

System details

01

Audience & promise

The live brand speaks to people across the United States through an entirely online model, with a strong connection to Brazilian and multilingual audiences. The communication is designed to reduce uncertainty and make the service experience feel easier to understand.

Brand promise: reliable, clear and caring support that remains accessible regardless of location.

02

Vision & mission

Prisma frames its role around hope, clarity and human support. The live website emphasizes making a complicated process feel simpler and more personal, while consistently reinforcing excellence, integrity, empathy and careful attention to each client's journey.

03

Voice & messaging

The brand combines professional authority with reassurance. It should sound capable without becoming institutional, and empathetic without becoming vague or sentimental.

  • Clear
  • Reassuring
  • Transparent
  • Human
  • Professional

Messaging principles

  • Translate complex information into straightforward language and visible next steps.
  • Acknowledge that the journey can feel stressful, then answer with calm confidence and practical support.
  • Reinforce precision, trust, transparency, care and attention to detail.
  • Present the digital model as convenient and flexible rather than distant or impersonal.
  • Keep calls to action simple: schedule a conversation, explore services, or contact the team.
04

Digital service model

The live experience brings services, reviews, team credibility, contact, scheduling, WhatsApp and social channels into one journey. The information hierarchy should quickly communicate what Prisma offers, how the remote model works, and how a visitor can begin a conversation.
